Our Sweet Story

Est. 1998 · Sathupally

A Mother's Recipe.
A Family's Promise.

It started in a small kitchen behind RTC Bus Stand — a mother's love, a grandmother's secret notes, and the smell of bread that made neighbours stop by. In 1998 we opened our doors. Our first customer cried when she tasted the bread. We knew we were home.

The Secret Isn't Secret

Fine ingredients, patient hands, and love. We tell the recipe — but the love you have to taste.
